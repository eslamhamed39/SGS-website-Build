Privacy Policy

Saudi Geological Survey

The Saudi Geological Survey (SGS) is a leading public authority specialized in geological sciences, earth sciences, and related disciplines. It provides government entities, the private sector, and the public with information, studies, and technical reports in these fields. It also works to prepare and create investment opportunities in the mining and natural resources sector to support the economy and job creation, including surveying, exploration, and prospecting for minerals and industrial rocks, preparing maps and reports, conducting geological and hydrological studies, monitoring natural phenomena such as earthquakes and volcanoes, and overseeing other geological risks, in addition to safeguarding the continuity of Zamzam water for the guests of God. Privacy Policy

We care about your privacy. This privacy notice helps you understand what information we collect and why we process it.

Updates to this policy

To keep offering the best experience and the highest level of protection for your personal data, we may update this policy (privacy notice) when necessary. We encourage you to review it periodically. The last update to this notice was in February 2025.

Data we collect

SGS collects the minimum personal data required, in line with the Personal Data Protection Law and its regulations in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including:

Personal data

Includes: name – national ID – residency number – passport number – date of birth – place of birth – gender – nationality – city of residence – country of residence – workplace – employment data – employment history – job title – appointment date – years of service – salary data – photo – family data – parents’ data.

Contact data

Includes: phone number – mobile number – national address – email address.

Cookies

Includes login data for accounts on SGS digital platforms, such as usernames and passwords. We also use cookies to improve service quality.

Purposes of collection and use

• Fulfilling SGS requirements to deliver requested services.
• Developing and improving service quality.
• Reviewing job applications for available positions.
• Communicating with beneficiaries who wish to receive services or inquire about them.
• Using data to improve services for users.
• Producing statistics based on types of content viewed or downloaded.
• Developing the website and services according to visitors’ needs.

How we collect and process personal data

We collect and process personal data obtained directly or indirectly through methods including:

Direct methods

• Services: when registering on official SGS channels and other services.
• Communication: when contacting SGS through its communication channels.
• Paper and electronic registration forms.
• Surveys.

Indirect methods

• Data provided by other government or private entities that support or deliver an SGS service.
• Cookies.

Disclosure of your personal data

SGS will not disclose your personal data without your consent except where the requesting entity is a public entity and disclosure is for public interest, security purposes, implementation of another law, or judicial requirements, in accordance with controls and procedures under the applicable Personal Data Protection Law in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Legal basis for processing

Under the Personal Data Protection Law, we rely on your explicit consent to process this data. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out under other legal bases.

Storage of personal data

Your personal data is stored securely and, where needed, encrypted on central storage systems. Retention follows defined retention periods, the purposes for which data was collected, and any legal, regulatory, or accounting requirements.

Retention and destruction

We retain personal data for the period required while the purpose of collection remains valid. When that purpose ends or upon your request, data is deleted securely so it cannot be recovered, subject to other applicable requirements in the Kingdom, without prejudice to exceptions under Article 18 of the Personal Data Protection Law. Data is destroyed after ten (10) years from the date of the last login.

Your rights regarding processing

The Personal Data Protection Law grants you the following rights:

• Right to be informed: to know the legal basis for collecting your data and the purpose of processing.
• Right of access: to access your personal data through channels provided by SGS.
• Right to obtain a copy: to receive a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used, readable electronic or printed format where possible.
• Right to rectification: to request correction, completion, or update of your data held by SGS, without affecting processing based on other legal bases.
• Right to destruction: to request destruction of personal data no longer needed, without prejudice to Article (18) of the Personal Data Protection Law.

Questions about this privacy notice?

For questions or requests regarding your rights or this notice, contact the Data Protection Officer at the Saudi Geological Survey at: dpo@sgs.gov.sa
